# Independent-label source review

We investigated replacements for the synthetic software-engineering selection dataset. We did not establish a publicly usable dataset that jointly supplies our technical assessment fields and independently recorded selection outcomes. This review is not proof that no such dataset exists.

| Source | What it supports | Why it cannot silently replace our dataset |
|---|---|---|
| https://arxiv.org/html/2504.05683v1 | HURIT mock HR transcripts and communication evaluation | Does not establish our software assessment fields and selection labels |
| https://roc-hci.com/past-projects/automated-prediction-of-job-interview-performances/ | MIT mock interview recordings and interview-performance research | Mock interview ratings are not actual software hiring outcomes |
| https://huggingface.co/datasets/Kidomakai/software-engineering-interview-practices-2005-2026 | Software interview-practice research | Interview experiences do not establish the required candidate-level prediction labels |

We retain explicit synthetic status. We do not rename communication ratings as coding scores, turn practice-survey responses into hiring labels, or present generated labels as observed decisions. An independently collected replacement needs documented input timing, stable candidate IDs, a scoring rubric and recorded outcomes with appropriate permission.
